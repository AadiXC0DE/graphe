**Typing `@` covered the window in somebody else's prose.** Each offer was drawn as a name on the left and its note on the right, and that note could not shrink. For a file the note is the folder it sits in, and it fitted. For a skill it is the whole description, so the row grew to fit a paragraph: several thousand pixels of it, out past the menu, out past the window, printed over everything behind it, with the name squeezed to nothing at the left edge. Twelve of those and the screen was unreadable, and not one row told you what the skill was called.

The note goes under the name now, where it was always meant to be, and it is clipped to one line. The name and the handle you are actually inserting sit above it. The menu keeps a width and a height, so twelve offers is a list you can scroll rather than a column taller than the window.
